Thesis Timeline Planner

Choose your degree and where you are in it. Before your proposal, the planner lays out a roadmap from your first term to your defence. After your proposal, it counts back from your defence to each thesis deadline.

Original idea by Jeffrey Wammes of the LAMP Lab.

Course planner (optional)

A typical plan is two courses in each fall and winter of Year 1. Year 2 courses are discouraged, but may be needed if a required course is not offered when you need it. Choose a MItHRIL course or enter another course, including MECH or CMPE. Check current offerings, descriptions, prerequisites and registration requirements on the ECE graduate courses page and confirm your plan with your supervisor.

Internship (optional)

An internship of 3 to 12 months pauses the thesis plan. Enter proposal and defence dates before the internship: planned events on or after its start are delayed until it ends. Confirm university deadlines, course availability, leave and extensions with your supervisor and department.
