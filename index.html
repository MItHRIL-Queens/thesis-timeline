Planned time away (optional)

Add your vacation (typically 10 business days a year) and any supervisor leave, such as a sabbatical or conference travel. The planner flags deadlines that fall inside these dates.

Setting expectations with your supervisor

These conversations come from the SGSPA Setting Expectations guidebook and the Productive Supervisory Relationships conversation guide. Go through them with your supervisor before you start writing, and again at the start of each academic year. Your ticks and notes stay in this browser and appear on the printout.
